You are asking UNTEPP to help you keep commitments you set for yourself. It is a prompt and an obstacle, not a lock.

  • Android may stop the app's background service at any time — some device manufacturers are notably aggressive about this — and a limit may then not be enforced when you expect it.
  • If Android or your device switches the Accessibility Service off, limits continue to be counted but apps are no longer held closed. UNTEPP tells you when it notices this, but it cannot turn the service back on for you.
  • Any limit can be dismissed, overridden, or removed by you, and the app can be uninstalled.
  • Some apps are never blocked, whatever your rules say: your phone and default messaging app, your home screen, your keyboard, Android's Settings and system screens, and UNTEPP itself. This is so that a rule can never lock you out of emergency calls, verification codes, or the means to change it.
  • Notifications such as reminders and weekly readings are delivered by Android and may arrive late or not at all, for example when notifications are turned off, quiet hours apply, or the device is saving battery. Do not rely on UNTEPP for anything time-critical.
  • Usage figures come from Android's own reporting and may differ from figures shown elsewhere on your device.
