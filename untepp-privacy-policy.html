Privacy Policy

Effective 9 September 2026 · Last updated 13 September 2026

UNTEPP is a digital wellbeing app for Android. It measures how you use your phone so you can decide to use it differently. The record of what you actually do on your device stays on your device.

The short version. UNTEPP has no accounts and no server of its own. Your usage history — which apps you open and for how long — is stored only in the app's local database on your phone and is never uploaded to us or to anyone else. What does leave the device is limited to anonymous product analytics, crash reports, your subscription status, and any feedback email you choose to send us.

1. Who we are

UNTEPP ("the app", "we", "us") is published by Chirag Prajapati, Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. For any privacy question or request, contact hello.untepp@gmail.com.

For users in the UK and EEA, we are the data controller for the limited personal data described in section 3.

2. No account, ever

UNTEPP does not ask you to register, sign in, or provide a name, email address, or phone number. There is nothing to log into. We therefore hold no directory of users and cannot connect app activity to your identity.

3. What the app collects, and where it goes

3.1 Stays on your device — always

The following never leaves your phone. It is written to a private database inside the app's own storage, which other apps cannot read:

  • App usage history — which apps you opened, when, and for how long, read through Android's Usage Access permission.
  • Your settings and rules — time limits, focus sessions, bedtime windows, reminders, goals and exclusions.
  • Your progress — streaks, badges, and the daily and weekly figures the app derives from the above.

We have no ability to retrieve any of this. There is no server it could be retrieved to.

This data is also excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, so it is not copied to your Google account or to a new phone. The only copy that leaves your device is an export you create yourself in the app's Data & privacy settings.

3.2 The Accessibility Service

To hold you to a limit at the moment you open an app, UNTEPP uses an Android Accessibility Service. It is worth being precise about what that does, because the permission sounds broader than the use:

  • It reads only the package name of the app that has just come to the foreground — for example com.example.social — and the type of window that opened (its class name, such as the name of an app screen, the keyboard, or the notification shade). The window type is used only to tell an app actually opening apart from a keyboard or system panel appearing over it.
  • It does not read, record, or transmit screen contents, text you type, passwords, messages, or anything shown in any app. The service is configured without the ability to retrieve window content.
  • The package name and window type are compared against your own rules on the device and then discarded. They are not logged and not sent anywhere.
  • The service takes one action: when an app covered by a limit, focus session or schedule you set comes to the front, it shows UNTEPP's pause screen over that app. It takes no other action on your device and changes no settings.
  • You are asked for explicit consent inside the app before being taken to Android's Accessibility settings, and you can turn the service off at any time in Settings > Accessibility.

3.4 Payments

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or receive your card number, bank details, or billing address. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt from Google in order to tell the app whether Premium is active.

4. Advertising identifier

Firebase Analytics may access the Android advertising ID on your device. UNTEPP shows no adverts, sells no data, and does not use this identifier for advertising or for cross-app tracking. You can reset or delete it at any time in Settings → Google → Ads on your device.

5. Legal basis (UK/EEA)

  • Contract — processing your subscription status, so the app you paid for works.
  • Legitimate interests — crash reporting and aggregate product analytics, to keep the app working and improve it. We consider this proportionate because the data is not tied to your identity and does not include your usage history.

Where consent is the applicable basis in your jurisdiction, you may withdraw it by uninstalling the app or by contacting us.

6. How long data is kept

  • On your device — until you delete it in the app or uninstall UNTEPP. Uninstalling removes the local database entirely.
  • Analytics and crash reports — retained by Google in line with Firebase's retention settings, up to 14 months.
  • Purchase records — retained by RevenueCat and Google Play for as long as needed to honour your entitlement and to meet tax and accounting obligations.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, and to data portability. Because the app holds no account:

  • Access and portability — everything the app knows about your usage is already on your device and can be exported to a PDF from within the app at any time.
  • Deletion — uninstalling the app deletes the local data. To request deletion of analytics or crash data, email us with the approximate date and your device model; note that because these records are not linked to your identity, we may be unable to locate them.
  • Purchases — subscription records are managed through your Google account; see Play subscriptions.

You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Sharing

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising. Data is shared only with the processors named in section 3.3, and where we are legally required to disclose it.

9. International transfers

Google and RevenueCat process data on servers that may be located outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards those providers offer (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).

10. Children

UNTEPP is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, note it in the app. Continuing to use UNTEPP after a change means you accept the updated policy.
