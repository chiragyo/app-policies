SensiTune is an offline-first utility that: scans basic device properties; generates suggested sensitivity values for supported mobile shooter games; lets you save, compare, convert, share and import profiles; provides a short on-screen Aim Test and a fine-tune assistant; and offers guides and an optional quick-view of your saved values.

The App does not read, modify, inject into, connect to, automate or interact with any game, and does not provide any aimbot, auto-aim, aim lock, auto-headshot, cheat, hack, mod, injector, FPS boost, RAM cleaning or lag-fixing function.
