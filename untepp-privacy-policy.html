What the app collects, and where it goes ### 3.1 Stays on your device — always The following never leaves your phone. It is written to a private database inside the app's own storage, which other apps cannot read: - **App usage history** — which apps you opened, when, and for how long, read through Android's Usage Access permission. - **Your settings and rules** — time limits, focus sessions, bedtime windows, reminders, goals and exclusions. - **Your progress** — streaks, badges, and the daily and weekly figures the app derives from the above. We have no ability to retrieve any of this. There is no server it could be retrieved to. ### 3.2 The Accessibility Service To hold you to a limit at the moment you open an app, UNTEPP uses an Android Accessibility Service. It is worth being precise about what that does, because the permission sounds broader than the use: - It reads **only the package name** of the app that has just come to the foreground — for example `com.example.social`. - It does **not** read, record, or transmit screen contents, text you type, passwords, messages, or anything shown in any app. The service is configured without the ability to retrieve window content. - The package name is compared against your own rules on the device and then discarded. It is not logged and not sent anywhere. ### 3.3 Leaves your device | What | Who processes it | Why | | --- | --- | --- | | **Anonymous product analytics** — that a focus session started or ended, a limit was created, the paywall was shown, a purchase started, succeeded, failed or was cancelled, onboarding finished, a permission was granted, an intervention was shown or overridden. Plus the device model, OS version, country, and an app-instance identifier assigned by Google. | Google (Firebase Analytics) | To see which features are used and where people get stuck. | | **Crash reports** — the error, the stack trace, device model, OS version, and app version at the time of a crash. | Google (Firebase Crashlytics) | To find and fix crashes. | | **Purchase and subscription status** — which plan was bought, when it renews or lapses, and an anonymous identifier RevenueCat generates for this installation. | RevenueCat, Inc. and Google Play | To unlock Premium and keep it unlocked across reinstalls. | > **Note what is not in that table.** The names of the apps you use, how long you use them, your limits, your streaks and your goals are not sent to Google, to RevenueCat, or to us. Analytics events record that something happened, never which of your apps it happened to. ### 3.4 Payments Purchases are handled entirely by **Google Play**. We never see or receive your card number, bank details, or billing address. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt from Google in order to tell the app whether Premium is active. ## 4. How long data is kept - **On your device** — until you delete it in the app or uninstall UNTEPP. Uninstalling removes the local database entirely. - **Analytics and crash reports** — retained by Google in line with Firebase's retention settings, up to 14 months. - **Purchase records** — retained by RevenueCat and Google Play for as long as needed to honour your entitlement and to meet tax and accounting obligations. ## 7. Your rights Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, and to data portability. Because the app holds no account: - **Access and portability** — everything the app knows about your usage is already on your device and can be exported to a PDF from within the app at any time. - **Deletion** — uninstalling the app deletes the local data. To request deletion of analytics or crash data, email us with the approximate date and your device model; note that because these records are not linked to your identity, we may be unable to locate them. - **Purchases** — subscription records are managed through your Google account; see [Play subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions). You may also complain to your local data protection authority. ## 8. Sharing We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.
