1. Who we are

SensiTune: Sensi & Aim Test (the "App") is published by Chirag Prajapati, an individual developer based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India ("we", "us", "our"). For the limited data described in section 4, we are the "data fiduciary" within the meaning of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), and Google acts as our processor.

2. Scope and your consent

This policy covers the App on Android. It does not cover Google Play, Google Firebase, your phone's manufacturer, the messaging apps you share to, or any game. Those are governed by their own policies.

By installing and using the App you agree to this policy and consent to the processing described in it. If you do not agree, please do not use the App and uninstall it. You can withdraw your consent at any time by uninstalling the App (see sections 11 and 12).

3. Data that stays on your phone

The App works offline. The following is stored only in the App's private storage on your phone and is not sent to us:

  • Device scan: phone model, screen size and resolution, refresh rate, memory size and whether a gyroscope is present. It is used only to calculate your sensitivity values, and you can edit it.
  • Your setup and profiles: fingers used, playstyle, gyro choice, selected game, and the profiles you save, rename, duplicate, convert or import.
  • Training data: Aim Test results (accuracy, overshoot, reaction time) and fine-tune adjustments.
  • Preferences: language, theme, and whether you completed the welcome screens.

This data leaves your phone only in the situations you control or that Android controls: when you share a profile (section 7) and when Android backs up your apps (section 8).

4. Data sent to Google Firebase

Release builds of the App use two Google Firebase services. They run only in the version downloaded from Google Play, not in developer builds.

ServiceWhat it collectsWhy
Firebase AnalyticsAnonymous events (for example app opened, screens viewed, features used), phone model, Android version, App version, language, approximate country (derived from IP address by Google) and a random app-instance ID.To understand which features are used and to improve the App.
Firebase CrashlyticsCrash reports: technical details of the crash, what the App was doing, phone model, Android version, App version and a random installation ID.To find and fix crashes.

This information is linked to a random installation ID, not to your name, email address, phone number or Google account. We do not try to identify you from it. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not build advertising profiles. The advertising ID is not collected: the App removes the advertising-ID permission and turns off ad personalisation signals.

The data is transmitted over HTTPS to Google. See Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Data we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address, phone number or password (the App has no sign-in).
  • No precise or approximate location from your device.
  • No contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera, SMS or call data.
  • No advertising ID, and no third-party advertising or ad-tracking SDKs.
  • No game data. The App never reads, modifies, connects to or automates any game, and does not read your screen or other apps.

6. Payments

The App is a paid download sold through Google Play. Payment, billing, tax and refunds are handled entirely by Google. We never receive or store your card or payment details. We may see aggregated sales and country-level reports provided by Google Play that do not identify you.

7. Sharing and importing profiles

When you choose to share a profile, the App creates an image and a short code containing your profile name and sensitivity values and hands it to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp or Discord). We do not receive or host what you share, and what happens next is governed by that app's policy. Please do not put personal information in profile names you plan to share. When you import a code, it is read and processed only on your phone.

8. Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may back up your saved profiles, Aim Test results and settings to your Google account and restore them on a new phone. This is performed by Google under your Google account settings; we do not have access to it. You can control it in your phone's system settings.

9. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only to send Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics data (and to open links such as the Play Store page).
  • Wake lock: declared by the Firebase library to deliver its reports reliably.
  • Picture-in-picture (quick-view): if you turn the quick-view on, your saved values appear in a small floating window. It only shows your saved values. It does not read the screen, send taps or input to another app, or detect which app is open. It needs no Accessibility Service, screen-capture or usage-access permission.

The App may check locally on your phone which store installed it. This check is performed on the device and the result is not sent to us.

10. Third parties and international transfers

Our only data processor is Google (Firebase). We do not sell, rent or trade personal data and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose information if required by law, court order or a lawful request from a government authority in India, or where needed to protect our legal rights.

Google may process Firebase data on servers outside India, including in the United States and other countries. By using the App you consent to this transfer. We have no control over, and give no assurance about, the data-protection law of those countries beyond the commitments Google makes in its terms.

11. Retention and deletion

  • On your phone: kept until you delete profiles, clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. Uninstalling removes everything stored on your phone by the App.
  • Firebase Analytics: retained by Google for up to 14 months.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: retained by Google for up to 90 days.

Because this data is tied only to a random installation ID, we can usually act on a deletion request only if you can help us identify that ID. To request deletion of data linked to your installation, email us at the address in section 17. We will respond within a reasonable time and, in any case, within the period required by applicable law.

12. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act and other applicable Indian law you may have the right to: access a summary of your personal data we process; request correction or erasure; withdraw consent; nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated; and have your grievances addressed. Contact us using section 17. You may also approach the Data Protection Board of India once it is constituted and the complaint procedure is in force, after first raising the matter with us.

If you are in another country (for example the EU, UK or Brazil) and local law gives you additional rights, you can use the same contact. We will honour them to the extent they apply to the very limited data in section 4.

13. Age and minors

The App is intended for users aged 16 and over and is not directed at children. Under Indian law, a person below 18 is a minor. If you are under 18, you may use the App only with the knowledge and consent of a parent or legal guardian, who accepts this policy on your behalf. We do not show ads, do not track or build behavioural profiles for advertising, and do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App, contact us and we will help with deletion where possible.

14. Security

We follow reasonable security practices: data in transit to Firebase is encrypted using HTTPS, and your profiles are stored in the App's private storage on your phone. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting your phone (screen lock, updates, and so on).
